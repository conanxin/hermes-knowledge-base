## Hook · 开场

> **EN** [00:00:01] All right, so how do you actually become financially free? Now, this might sound abundantly obvious, but there are a few hidden qualities across the people that actually make it happen.

> **中文** [00:00:01] 好,那么你究竟怎样才能实现财务自由?这个答案听起来可能显而易见,但那些真正做到的人身上其实有几条隐秘特质。

---

## Definition · 财务自由定义

> **EN** [00:01:30] My wallet is full. But I work because I want to, not because I have to.

> **中文** [00:01:30] 我的钱包已经满了。但我工作是因为我想工作,不是因为不得不工作。

---

## Hours per week · 真实工作时长的反差

> **EN** [00:01:25] I work about 20% of what I used to. Roughly one day a week.

> **中文** [00:01:25] 我现在工作的时间大概是过去的 20%,差不多一周一天。

---

## Asset · 资产 vs 工作

> **EN** [00:02:00] Assets earn while you sleep. Income stops the moment you stop working.

> **中文** [00:02:00] 资产在你睡觉时也赚钱;工作在你停下的瞬间就停了。

---

## Leverage · 复合 vs 线性

> **EN** [00:03:18] Time is your enemy on income. Time is your friend on assets.

> **中文** [00:03:18] 在工薪收入上,时间是敌人;在资产型收入上,时间是朋友。

---

## Roadmap · 商业路线图的时间窗

> **EN** [00:04:13] Is there a 5-10 year version of this?

> **中文** [00:04:13] 有没有一条 5–10 年就能到的路?

---

## Energy · 管能量不是管时间

> **EN** [00:06:31] We're not in the business of time management. We're in the business of energy management.

> **中文** [00:06:31] 我们不是在做时间管理的生意,我们是在做能量管理的生意。

---

## Notion · Notion 12 小时/周

> **EN** [00:07:15] Our whole company was built on twelve hours a week.

> **中文** [00:07:15] 我们整个公司是建立在一周 12 小时之上的。

---

## Systems · 系统 > 意志力

> **EN** [00:07:38] Willpower is a finite resource. If you rely on it, you'll fail.

> **中文** [00:07:38] 意志力是有限的资源。如果单纯依赖它,你会失败。

---

## Hard mode vs Easy mode

> **EN** [00:19:00] Hard mode is doing SaaS, hiring a team, opening a coffee shop. Easy mode is services, digital products, education.

> **中文** [00:19:00] 困难模式是 SaaS、组建团队、开咖啡店;简单模式是服务、数字产品、教育。

---

## Pareto · 80/20 应用

> **EN** [00:23:00] One client worth £1000 beats ten clients worth £100.

> **中文** [00:23:00] 1 个愿意付 1000 英镑的客户胜过 10 个愿意付 100 英镑的。

---

## Value first · 免费先送价值

> **EN** [00:29:50] "Hey, I'm a student. I'd love to take you out for coffee."

> **中文** [00:29:50] "你好,我是个学生。我想请你喝杯咖啡聊聊。"

---

## Closing · 收尾

> **EN** [00:58:30] Financial freedom isn't about not working. It's about choosing what you work on and who you work with.

> **中文** [00:58:30] 财务自由不是"不工作"。是你能选择做什么、和谁一起做。
